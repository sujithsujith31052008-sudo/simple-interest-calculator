# Simple Interest Calculator

## Description

A Simple Interest Calculator calculates the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specified period at a given rate of interest.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* **P** = Principal amount
* **R** = Annual rate of interest
* **T** = Time in years

## Example

* Principal: ₹10,000
* Rate of Interest: 5% per annum
* Time: 2 years

Calculation:

SI = (10000 × 5 × 2) / 100

**Simple Interest = ₹1,000**

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

**Total Amount = ₹11,000**

## Features

* Calculates simple interest.
* Calculates the total amount payable.
* Easy to understand and use.
